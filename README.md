# Fast Food Simulator

This is a game made by Jonathan Sandström. The game is about working in a fast food restaurant, where the player will make food as fast as possible. 

This game is largely inspired by No Ceiling Games' Fast Food Simulator on Steam. They were mostly liked for their gameplay and multiplayer experience. You could start as a beginner in the Fast Food Simulator game and gain good experience. However, the good experience only lasts until level 14. At level 14, especially in singleplayer mode, the player needs to handle multiple errands at the same time (e.g., drive-thru, then making hamburgers, then making fries, making soda, changing priorities, etc.). This type of complexity forces the player to parallel tasks in the kitchen, which becomes frustrating to the players and stops playing. In regard to the organization's (No Ceiling Games) response to these issues, they have made a difficult choice not to provide feedback from players and continue finishing the game as far as they could. As far as the Steam reviews go, new reviews of the game are now mostly mixed. This signifies a change in the organization that has had a negative impact on most of the players, especially in the Fast Food Simulator game.

This is more of a personal project for me. I want to make this game because it is fun, but not as fun as when the game is unbalanced in the game flow. When I was playing their game, I found lots of issues, not just bugs, but mostly about balancing the game mechanics. Even though I wanted to see the changes from the developers, they won't want to sit around with the players and give feedback anymore, as the developers were mostly concerned about how much code they needed to refactor. Soon enough, it was clear to me that they wouldn't fix the balance of the game anytime soon. That is when I decided to initialize this project. I wanted to make it fun not just for me, but also for the players, of whom wants to experience what working on a fast food job ***ACTUALLY*** looks like. Not only that, but I want to try to beat the Fast Food Simulator game on Steam, and make the players' stay longer.

Maybe in the future, No Ceiling Games will reconsider Fast Food Simulator and make it into a different form. For now, it is kind of bleak. I will try my best to make this game happen. Maybe I'll make it onto Steam someday, but for now this is just a proof of concept.
